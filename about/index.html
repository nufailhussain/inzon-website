About INZON

Rethinking corporate gifts for modern workplaces

Last updated: 26 November 2026

INZON Times and Gifts is a Kerala-based wholesale supplier and private label partner for corporate gifts and branded merchandise. INZON designs, sources and brands gift sets, drinkware, bags, apparel, clocks and lifestyle products, and supplies decor items for offices, homes and showrooms, for companies, retail chains and institutions across India and the GCC.

Our story

How did INZON start?

INZON Times and Gifts began with market research and a simple aim: to offer gift products that look and feel different from what buyers usually find. Instead of repeating standard catalogue items, INZON set out to bring new product ideas, fresh designs and practical solutions to corporate gifting.

The INZON team visits domestic and international markets to track new trends, then turns the best ideas into products of its own. This steady cycle of research and product development is how INZON builds a range that buyers do not see everywhere else.

That focus on new ideas and consistent quality has helped INZON build working relationships with leading companies, established retail chains and large business organisations, including Lulu Hypermarket, Nesto Hypermarket, Daymart, SWA Diamonds and Mingler, and the list keeps growing. Today INZON works as a wholesale supplier and private label partner, not only as a trader.

What we offer

What does INZON do?

INZON supplies branded corporate gifts and decor items in bulk, sources custom products to your design, and supplies goods under your own brand name. Each product is chosen with attention to design, everyday use, buyer preferences and quality.

  • Corporate gifting

    Gift sets, bottles, bags, apparel, pens, clocks and more, branded with your logo for teams, clients and events.

  • Custom products

    Products sourced to your specification, from a new concept or an existing design, in the volumes your business needs.

  • Private label solutions

    Products sourced and packed under your own brand name, so they carry your identity instead of a supplier's.

  • Bulk and wholesale supply

    Reliable large-volume supply for retail chains and businesses, with a minimum order of 100 units for branded gifts.

Decor items

Does INZON supply decor items?

Yes. Decor is INZON's second vertical, next to corporate gifts. INZON sources decor items from manufacturers and suppliers and supplies them in bulk to offices, showrooms, hospitality spaces, retailers and businesses fitting out new spaces.

The decor range covers wall art and mirrors, macrame wall hangings, table decor, table lamps, artificial plants, planters and vases, key holders, and storage and organisers such as laundry baskets, clothes racks and drying stands. The same terms apply as for gifts: minimum 100 units, full payment before delivery and delivery across Kerala.

Sourcing and quality

How does INZON source its products?

INZON sources products from trusted manufacturers and suppliers, and checks them for quality before they are branded and packed. This keeps every piece consistent from the first to the last, and lets INZON handle large-volume orders.

INZON keeps growing its network of manufacturers and suppliers so that business partners get what they expect today and what they will need next.

Custom products and private label

Can INZON supply products under my brand?

Yes. INZON sources products that reflect your brand, from custom designs and product concepts to finished goods under your own brand name. Every brand has its own identity, so each project is planned around your requirements.

Bring an existing product design or a completely new idea. The INZON team will work with you to turn it into a finished product.
