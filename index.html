FAQs

Questions buyers ask INZON

These answers cover minimum order quantity, lead time, mockups, branding methods, GST invoicing, delivery and artwork files. For anything not listed, message INZON on WhatsApp or request a quote.

What is INZON?

INZON is a B2B corporate gifting, promotional merchandise and decor supplier based in Kozhikode (Calicut), Kerala, India. INZON supplies custom-branded gift sets, drinkware, bags, apparel, pens, keychains, caps, umbrellas, wall clocks and home products, plus a range of decor items, to companies, institutions and event teams that order in bulk. INZON does not sell to individual consumers.

Does INZON supply decor items?

Yes. Alongside corporate gifts, INZON supplies decor items for offices, homes, showrooms and hospitality spaces: wall art and mirrors, macrame wall hangings, table decor, table lamps, artificial plants, planters and vases, key holders, and storage and organisers. Decor orders follow the same terms as gifts: minimum 100 units, full payment before delivery and delivery across Kerala. Ask when you request a quote whether a decor item can carry your logo.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom corporate gifts at INZON?

The minimum order quantity at INZON is 100 units. Share your product, quantity and branding needs when you request a quote, and INZON confirms the price and any product-specific details for each item before you commit to the order.

How long does a bulk corporate gift order take?

A typical INZON order takes TO CONFIRM: lead time in working days from artwork approval to dispatch. The time depends on the product, quantity and branding method, and on stock of the base product. If you have a fixed event date, tell INZON when you request a quote so the schedule can be planned backwards from that date.

Can I see my branded product before production?

Yes. INZON prepares a digital mockup showing your logo on the product, with placement and colours, and production starts only after you approve it in writing. A physical sample is available for many products TO CONFIRM: sample policy and any sample charge, which is useful for large orders or new product types.

Which branding methods does INZON offer?

INZON offers branding methods matched to each material: TO CONFIRM: list, e.g. screen printing, laser engraving, embroidery, UV printing, sublimation and debossing. Laser engraving suits metal pens, bottles and keychains. Embroidery suits caps and polos. Screen or UV printing suits bags, umbrellas and clock faces. INZON recommends the method when quoting.

Does INZON provide a GST invoice?

INZON issues a GST invoice for business orders under GSTIN 32ABRFM8059P1ZC, so your company can claim input tax credit where eligible. Share your company name, billing address and GSTIN when you confirm the order. Payment terms are 100% payment before delivery.

Where does INZON deliver in Kerala?

INZON delivers to all of Kerala. Orders can go to one office or be split across several addresses. Delivery charges and timelines depend on the destination and are shown in your quote.

What artwork file do I need to send for branding?

Send your logo as a vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) for the sharpest printing, engraving or embroidery. A high-resolution PNG can work for some methods. Include your brand colour codes (Pantone or HEX) if colour matching matters. INZON checks the file and tells you if anything needs to change before the mockup.
